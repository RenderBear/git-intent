# Intent land

Use this optional semantic guide when a candidate needs architectural judgment. The `invariant` CLI
owns candidate construction, reach recomputation, verifier selection, detached-tree checks, lease
authentication, atomic ref updates, synchronization, and cleanup.

Review the exact prospective tree, not a cached brief or an earlier diff. For every affected
architecture section, decide whether the candidate preserves the decision. Confirm one durable
boundary disposition: `no-record`, a fresh conclusive `audit:<id>`, or `recorded` with accepted
governance references. Removing or contradicting accepted meaning remains gated.

When outcome review is enabled, separately assess every required task acceptance ID as `satisfied`,
`not-satisfied`, or `unresolved`, with prose and evidence bound to the candidate tree. This optional
review does not replace preservation of existing architecture and contracts.

Supply the resulting assessment through `invariant task finish` or use `invariant candidate verify`
for read-only inspection. Report durable-meaning impact, reviewed decisions, checks, and the local
landing result. Push, deployment, publication, and destructive cleanup require separate authority.
